Approach

A small studio for one strong character.

Studio Mäntylä takes mascots, character stills, and short animations. The work stays with a single sculptor from the first sketch to the final frames.

What a commission looks like

  1. 01 · Brief

    You share the world the character belongs to, and the smallest place it has to read — a favicon, a label, a phone screen.

  2. 02 · Silhouette

    Two or three directions, then one sculpt. You react to the clay before materials and light.

  3. 03 · Finish

    Lookdev, a lit still or a turnaround, and motion only if the character needs to move.
